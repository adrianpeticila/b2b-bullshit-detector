# The B2B Bullshit Detector

A client-side Vanilla JS tool that scans B2B hero copy and ruthlessly flags corporate jargon. Built for [peticila.ro](https://peticila.ro/hydra).

## Why this exists

B2B landing pages are plagued by meaningless corporate jargon. We "synergize", "leverage", and shift "paradigms" instead of simply telling the customer what pain we eliminate. This tool forces clarity by catching the fluff before it ships.

## How it works

1. **Zero Server Contact:** The entire tool runs in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.
2. **Regex Dictionary:** It scans your input against a curated dictionary of the worst offenders in corporate speak (e.g., *synergize, leverage, next-gen, paradigm*).
3. **Harsh Truth:** It gives you a bullshit score and tells you exactly how many buzzwords you used, offering a StoryBrand-style fix to rewrite your copy.
